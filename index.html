About Us

YOUR CAR,
OUR CRAFT.

Satnam Auto — formerly Stewart Auto Electric — has been part of Delta's community for decades. Under Satnam's leadership, the shop has become the go-to for drivers across the Fraser Valley who want honest work at honest prices.

Our team — led by Karan (Karn) and Kam — brings genuine expertise to every job. We're a BC Designated Inspection Facility, meaning we're licensed and certified to perform official safety inspections.

We don't upsell. We don't charge for what you don't need. We explain every repair in plain language and only do what your car actually requires. That's why our customers keep coming back — and keep sending their friends.
